# GTDTracker Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 5, 2026_

GTDTracker ("the app") is an unofficial companion app for tracking your own GITADORA play and petal spending on Project Flower (projectflower.eu). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Konami or Project Flower.

**Summary:** the app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Everything it stores stays on your device unless you choose to export it.

## What the app stores on your device

- **Project Flower identifiers** you enter on the Settings screen: your account ID, GITADORA profile ID and petal wallet ID.
- **Your play and spending history** downloaded from your Project Flower account: song titles, play times, difficulty ratings, skill scores and instrument, plus petal transactions (date, source, description, amount and balance).
- **Statistics** the app calculates from that history (games, card loads, cost per game).
- **Your Project Flower login session.** You sign in on Project Flower's own login page, shown inside the app. The app never reads or stores your password; the session cookie the site issues is kept in the app's private storage so it can fetch your history.

The developer never receives any of this data.

## Network connections

The app connects only to Project Flower (`projectflower.eu` and `kailua.projectflower.eu`), over HTTPS, and only to sign you in and to download your own history when you tap **Sync now**. Those requests are handled by Project Flower under its own privacy policy. The app does not contact any other server, including any server operated by the developer.

## Exports and backups

- **Export/import:** you can export your data to a JSON file at a location you choose with Android's file picker, and import it again later. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.
- **Android backup:** if you have Android's backup service turned on, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account, under Google's terms. You can turn this off in your device settings.

## Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking libraries.

## Deleting your data

- Use **Clear Scores** and **Clear Transactions** on the Settings screen to delete your stored history.
- Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it has stored, including your login session.
- Data held by Project Flower is not affected; contact Project Flower to manage your account there.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
